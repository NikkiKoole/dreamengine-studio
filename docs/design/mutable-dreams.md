# Mutable Dreams: what it is, and what porting it costs us

**STATUS: BUILDING (2026-11-09)** — studied the v1.4 source; the biome atlas, the sequencer and the
Lo-Fi kit are PORTED as the `dreamatlas` cart; the fifteen voices are MAPPED onto shipped engines
(no new DSP), except the KICK and SNARE, which are Plaits' own circuits ported into the cart. MEASURED against
upstream's own engine compiled natively (§Measured): drum/breath tone fixed, per-voice level trims
fitted. Open: Rings' brightness, the hat's (2×), a listen.

Upstream: <https://github.com/kidbee85-beep/mutable-dreams> tag `v1.4` (commit `0321551`), by
Kid Bunka / Christoph Link. Play it: <https://mutable-dreams.kidbee85.chatgpt.site/>.

## What it is

Free firmware for the **M-VAVE FM-1** (a small groovebox, 240×240 screen) on the
[fm1-base](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-base) platform. Six monophonic tracks; each picks one
of fifteen voice models and plays its OWN Euclidean rhythm (`hits` over `length`, `rotate`, with a
per-step probability, a speed divisor, ratchets and an accent). Track lengths differ (16, 19, 13…),
so the tracks drift against each other. Everything feeds a granular "tape" (four grains over a 3 s
buffer, freeze/reverse/pitch), a delay and a four-line room. A **biome** is one saved scene of all
that; 157 ship (156 + Blank), in ten families (Mist, Drone, Resonance, Dub & Techno, Broken Pulses,
Organic, Rhythm Lab, Light & Melody, Blank, Lo-Fi), browsed in a curated order that puts unlike
neighbours side by side.

The whole engine is ONE file, `app/dsp/dreams.cpp` (947 dense lines), plus vendored Plaits, Rings
and Elements sources.

**Licence.** The combined distribution is GPL-3.0-only, but every file we take from is dual or
permissive: `dreams.cpp` and `generate_lofi.py` are `MIT OR GPL-3.0-only`, the preset atlas
(`preset_bank.inc`, from `tools/generate_presets.py`) is MIT, and Émilie Gillet's Plaits/Rings/
Elements are MIT. We take them as **MIT, with credit** (cart `de:meta` lineage + generated header).

## What we took, and how

| Upstream | Here | How |
|---|---|---|
| `presets/catalog.json` (the browser copy of the flash atlas) | `runtime/dreamatlas/biomes.h` | GENERATED by `tools/gen-dreamatlas.js` — 157 × (scene fields + six `md_track` rows) + the browse order. The catalog is not committed: `node tools/gen-dreamatlas.js --fetch` downloads it at the pinned commit into `build/` and regenerates (`--fetch --check` = the staleness gate; `--fetch <tag>` for a newer release) |
| `scene()` — fx sends by family, chord journeys, the Velvet Keys phrases (ids 77–108), the atlas archetypes' lead phrases and kit (ids ≥ 109) | `scene()` in the cart | hand-ported: it is code, not data |
| `tick()` — the step clock, the 32-step seed lock, Euclid hits, the density macro (`macro[0]`) and the scale-jump macro (`macro[1]`), ratchets, accents | `tick()` | ported line by line, booked on the audio clock (`schedule_note_at`) |
| `md_quantize`, `md_hit`, `chord_offset` + their 13 scale masks | same names | verbatim |
| `tools/generate_lofi.py` (5 banks × 8 pads, 16 kHz) + `lofi_kit.inc` (player + 4 groove masks) | `kit_render()` / `kit_pattern()` | ported; the player's linear read, one-pole tone filter and decay are BAKED into the PCM at load (the factory biomes never change pad settings), then played by `INSTR_SAMPLE` |

What a biome is NOT carrying: `ext[]` (bass mode, marbles, glide, warps) is always at its defaults
in the factory atlas, because upstream's `FactoryPatch` stops before it — so those features exist for
the user's edits and not for any shipped scene. The cart ignores them.

## Voices

The experiment is whether our shelf already covers it. It does, with two loose fits.

| Upstream model | What it actually is | Mapped to | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| KICK / SNARE / HAT | Plaits analog drums (808-style) | `INSTR_SINE` + pitch env / noise band + tri body / `INSTR_METAL` | ✅ character, not bit-exact |
| FM | Plaits 2-op FM, ratio · index · feedback | `INSTR_FM`, the same three macros | ✅ |
| RINGS | Rings modal resonator, struck by a damped mallet | `INSTR_MODAL`, `MODE_MODAL_MAP` = Elements route | ✅ (their damping knob means LENGTH; ours means damping, so `morph = 1 − decay`) |
| ELEMENTS | Elements exciter (mallet / plectrum / particles) into the resonator | `INSTR_MODAL`, exciter on `MODE_MODAL_EXCITE` | ✅ |
| VELVET KEYS | 2-op phase modulation, ratio 1–4, the index falling after the strike | `INSTR_FM` + `ENV_TIMBRE` | ✅ |
| CHOIR | three sines voicing a triad (`harm` picks the chord), detuned by `shape` | three `INSTR_SINE` notes with per-note cents | ✅ |
| BASS / MELLOW | sine fundamental + sub / two drifting sines + a finite odd-harmonic triangle | `INSTR_SINE`/`TRI`, a sub layer / unison detune | ✅ |
| BREATH | lowpassed noise crossfaded with a sine reed | sine slot + filtered `INSTR_NOISE` layer | ✅ |
| SATIN / SPECTRAL | two detuned oscillators morphing four band-limited single cycles | `INSTR_WAVESCAN` (its dark half) | ◐ a different table; theirs (`generate_satin.py`, MIT) could go into `wave_set` |
| VA | Plaits VA: two variable-shape oscillators, saw ↔ pulse ↔ hard sync on one knob | `INSTR_SAW` + unison + `instrument_sync` past half-tone | ◐ **the first gap**: we have no continuous variable-shape oscillator |
| HARMONIC | Plaits additive: a moving "bump" over 24 harmonics | `INSTR_ORGAN` (snapped drawbar recipes) | ◐ **the second gap**: no continuous spectral bump |

The per-track chain (exponential envelope → LP12 / LP24 / low-pass gate → pan → sends) and the
modulation (Breathe = a 7–20 s sine per track; Tides on ambient tracks) are all shipped pieces.
Breathe and Tides are applied PER NOTE (`NoteOpts.cutoff_oct`, sampled at the note's time), where
upstream rides them continuously: for a 7–20 s drift on notes a fraction of that long it is the same
gesture.

**If the gaps matter**, both are straight MIT ports from Plaits (`dsp/oscillator/variable_shape_oscillator.h`,
`dsp/engine/additive_engine.cc`), sized like any new `INSTR_*` (the four-place API rule +
`tune-check`). Usage says they are not urgent: across the 157 biomes HARMONIC is on 127 tracks — the
most used model — but mostly as a quiet pad colour; VA is on 68.

## Where the port is looser than the source

- **Grain tape.** Upstream feeds the tape PER TRACK (`send`) and plays it back scaled by `macro[3]`.
  We run the master `grains()` insert with one mix (average send × `macro[3]`). A per-slot
  `instrument_grains` would be truer and costs six buffers.
- **Ducking.** Upstream ducks every track except the source; `sidechain(0, …)` ducks the master,
  the kick included (the precedent other carts accept, see acidcandy's PUMP).
- **Level.** Upstream's mix runs ~6 dB hotter (`×1.9` then a soft clip). Levels were set by
  measurement (`wav-analyze` over a cross-section of biomes: peaks −9 to −19 dBFS, no clipping), not
  matched to the original.
- **Gated sequence notes** use the slot's decaying envelope over the gate length; upstream HOLDS the
  envelope through the gate, then releases in ~9 ms.

## Measured against the original (2026-11-09)

We no longer need the browser build to compare: `bash tools/ref-render/run.sh mutable` compiles
upstream's OWN engine (`dreams.cpp` + the vendored Plaits/Rings/Elements, exactly fm1.toml's source
list) natively as `build/ref-render/mdrender`, which renders any biome, any track alone, dry or wet.
`node tools/ref-render/mdcompare.js <id>` renders the same track through both engines and prints
level · crest · duty · centroid side by side; `--fit <ids…>` turns a set of compared biomes into a
per-model LEVEL RESIDUAL against the common offset between the two engines. The cart's twins of the
flags are `-DDA_ID=<id> -DDA_SOLO=<n> -DDA_DRY`. Never sample-aligned (upstream steps at sample 0, we
at ~0.1 s): these are character numbers, not a null test.

First fit, 20 biomes (ids 0 4 11 12 3 19 33 34 41 42 2 49 56 57 101 26 77 109 112 116), 98 sounding
tracks, ours +6.7 dB overall (kept: ours is set by our own level measurements):

| model | level residual | centroid ours/theirs | what it was, what changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| KICK | **+22 dB → +3.4** | 0.23× → 0.86× | Plaits' analog kick at notes 24-36 is a quiet sub under a loud click; ours was a loud 32 Hz sine, trimmed −22 at first. Now PORTED instead of mapped: `kick_render()` runs Plaits' `AnalogBassDrum` (1 ms pulse → band-pass SVF, pulse leaking out through a one-pole at 4·f0·2^(9·tone), attack + self FM) once per track into a sample slot, played by `INSTR_SAMPLE`. Re-fit over its 11 tracks: trim +3.4, crest within 1 dB, octave spectrum within ~3 dB |
| SNARE | +2.5 → −5.0 | 6× → ~3× → 0.97× | first mapped (noise × snappy over a triangle body), which got it to ~3× but our 2-pole LOW-pass left a long bright tail. Now PORTED: `snare_render()` runs Plaits' `AnalogSnareDrum` (five SVF body modes, soft clip, half-wave noise BAND-passed at 16·f0) into a sample like the kick. Re-fit over 9 tracks: trim +5.0, crest within ~2 dB. Four noise draws per track, packed into one sample and rotated hit by hit over four slots (`SL_ROUND`): hit-to-hit correlation 0.96 vs upstream's 0.92 (upstream also carries the body's ring over between hits) |
| HAT | −3.9 | 6× → 2× | the filter was a 5-9 kHz HIGH-pass; Plaits band-passes at 150·2^(6·tone) Hz (~500 Hz). Fixed |
| BREATH | −3.2 | 8× → 1× | air was a 7 kHz LP; theirs is a one-pole at 56 + 772·tone² Hz. Fixed |
| HARMONIC | **−9.4** | 0.99× | brightness right, sits ~9 dB low. Trimmed. The "loose voice" is not loose in timbre by this measure |
| FM | −6.0 | 1.02× | trimmed |
| CHOIR | +5.8 | 0.83× | trimmed |
| VA / VELVET | −2.7 / −3.3 | 1.02× / 0.91× | trimmed. VA, the other "loose voice", also matches in brightness |
| RINGS | +1.7 | **0.41×** | level fine; consistently ~2.5× DARKER than Rings. Untouched: the next voice to look at |
| BASS / ELEMENTS / MELLOW / SATIN / SPECTRAL | small, or n < 3 | | left at 0 until a sweep covers them |

The trims live in the cart's `VOICE_TRIM_DB[15]`, with the fit they came from.

## Our own biomes

The cart appends biomes of its own after the 157 factory ones (`DA_OWN_BIOME[]` in the cart, ids from
157, browsed last, tagged OURS; `biomes.h` stays generated and untouched). Same row format, same
sequencer, same randomness. The first is **Marmalade Jam**: a standard four-on-the-floor at 125 in G
major (kick 4/16, offbeat hat, clap on 2 and 4, a bass rotated off every kick, tresillo keys), I-vi-IV-V
two bars a chord, the kick ducking the rest. One odd length for the whimsy: a Rings pluck at 5 in 13.

It also carries the one thing no factory biome has, borrowed from Playtime's drum jam
(`playtime/docs/DRUM-JAM.md`): a live ARRANGER on 8-bar phrases (`jam_phrase()`). Layers enter one
phrase at a time (kick + hat, bass, clap, keys, lead), then a layer may rest a phrase, or a breakdown
takes kick, clap and bass out and the next phrase drops them all back. The last bar of each phrase is a
fill: the clap runs up its last steps (a rising roll out of a breakdown), the hats let go before the one.
Its rolls use its own rng, so the tracks' chance rolls are exactly the factory sequencer's.

The second is **Lantern Bazaar**, hand percussion on two engines Mutable Dreams never had, added as
models 16-17 for our biomes only: **TABLA** (`INSTR_MEMBRANE`: harm = head, tone = strike position,
shape = the bayan's pitch swoop, decay = ring length 55 ms-1.8 s) and **SCRAPE** (`INSTR_SCRAPE`: harm
= body gourd/bone/metal, tone = tooth rate, shape = steady scrape to bouncing rattle, decay = stroke
length 40-800 ms). A low tabla 3 in 16 and a high one 7 in 16 (tuned, wandering with the jump macro),
a guiro on the tresillo, a round bass, a Rings marimba 5 in 12, and a quijada rattling once every two
bars. D dorian at 100, i-IV-i-v. Each of our biomes carries a `JamCfg`: the order its layers enter,
who may rest, who a breakdown removes, which track plays the fill (here the high tabla) and which lets
go before the one.

The third, **Moon Garden**, came from playing Moon Meadow next to the Bazaar's tablas: Moon Meadow
(factory id 10) with its choir pad, 2-in-19 harmonic drone and Rings kept exactly (C dorian, 68 BPM,
family Mist, so its ambient sends and tides), and the other three tracks swapped for a low tabla, a
high tabla and a soft fading scrape. Its jam starts on the mist, adds the percussion one phrase at a
time, and its breakdown takes only the percussion away. The bed is ~4.5 dB under Moon Meadow's own
levels so the percussion peaks sit just beneath it.

The fourth, **Slow Brew**, is Playtime's brew style (`playtime/src/music-brew.lua`, after Ronnie
Foster) broken into cycles on brew's OWN instruments, four more models copied from Playtime's
`synth.lua` patches: **UPRIGHT** (`hUpright`: the bowed engine finger-plucked, dark, mono), **ORGAN**
(`brewOrgan`: thin tonewheel registration, 1.2 kHz, a slow Leslie), **GUITAR** (`brewGuitar`) and
**BELL** (`brewBell`, played as a high/low agogo pair); the kick is `bapKick` on the drumhead model.
Brew's key, tempo and one-chord-a-bar i-III-VI (a `JamCfg` can now carry its own chord cycle, here 3
bars long) but deliberately NOT its riff: the upright is 13 hits over 24 eighths, a 3-bar cycle like
the riff's, wandering in the scale. The organ voices each chord ROOTLESS (3-5-7-9: Fm9, Abmaj9,
Dbmaj9, checked from the trace) every three beats, the guitar strums on a 10-eighth cycle, the agogo
plays 5 in 12. Its jam builds like brew's intro (drums, bells, then the band) and its breakdown is
brew's break: drums and bells alone. `JamCfg.names[]` relabels the tracks (the drumhead reads KICK).

The fifth, **Mipo Band**, is Puppetmaker 2's band (playtime `docs/PUPPETMAKER-SOUND.md` §1a, its
`puppetmaker` style and patches): 60 BPM in 16ths, swing 52, chromatic, nothing resolves. Three more
models: **TAMB** (noise high-passed at 6.5 kHz) and **RHODES** (the `rhodes` EPIANO patch, playing
babirhodes' rhodes2 as measured: ONE rolled six-note chord, root + 0 7 16 19 21 24, up-strummed +29
+23 +23 +76 +40 ms, ringing 3 s, at a wandering root). The conga and the two bongos (one track,
alternating low/high via `JamCfg.pair[]`; Slow Brew's agogo pair uses the same) are the conga patch
on the drumhead, the guiro is the scrape, and the ringing triangle (`triangleRing`, 1.8 s, on 6 and 14)
is the bell model. The Mipos' singing (pi/po, the odd ba/bi, on `INSTR_VOICE`) was built and CUT: it
sounded awful.

The sixth, **CR-78 Bossa**, is the CR-78's own BOSSA NOVA preset on the voices of the `cr78` cart
(rebuilt from the circuit, fitted to real CR-78 one-shots), copied in as models **CR KICK**, **CR RIM**
(the click + its faint knock on the layer slot) and **MARACAS**, plus **NYLON** (the bossa cart's
PLUCK guitar). The kick `X.....x.X.....x.` and the rimshot clave `x..x..x...x..x..` are EXACT: a
`JamCfg.pat[]` fixed pattern rides the phrase sequencer (each step still rolls the track's chance).
The nylon comps on that same clave (it IS João's comping pattern at half speed) in rootless 3-7-9
voicings (`JamCfg.gtr`; checked from the trace: Gm9 Bb-F-A, C9 E-Bb-D, Fmaj9 A-E-G), the upright plays
the surdo (root and fifth with the kick), ii-V-I-vi in F at 128, two bars a chord. The cycles are
the maracas, 10 in 12 against the bar, and a Velvet keys melody 3 in 10.

The seventh, **Drift Garden**, composes itself (`JamCfg.evolve`, `evolve_step()`): it starts sparse
in D major pentatonic (a drum 3 in 16, a shaker 5 in 12, Rings 3 in 7, a bass 2 in 9, Velvet keys 2 in
11, a bell 1 in 13) and every 4 bars makes ONE mutation from its own rng: a hit more or less, a
rotation, a cycle a step longer or shorter (5..16), a melody note a scale step or two (at most a fifth
from home), a tone nudge, or a CANON (a track takes another's rhythm, a step behind). A quarter of the
time it instead steps one changed value back toward HOME (`S.home`, the planted biome): the rubber
band that lets it walk without dissolving. At least three tracks always sound. The screen shows the
generation and the last move. A 10-minute render made 52 moves, e.g. the drum growing 3→6 hits, the
bell going silent and returning, the bass echoing the drum, the rubber band pulling the bass home.
Because a biome is 153 numbers, any generation is a ~500-character snapshot: the obvious next step is
to show or save the current genome so a moment of the walk can be kept or shared.

**The world atlas** (Clave, Bembe and Aksak Atlas) is built only from rhythms Godfried Toussaint showed
are exactly Euclidean ("The Euclidean Algorithm Generates Traditional Musical Rhythms", 2005), each
track named for its rhythm with its origin under it (`JamCfg.from[]`). The rotation of our `md_hit`
that reproduces each traditional form was found by enumeration (the bembe bell `x.x.xx.x.x.x` is
rotation 7 of E(7,12), the cinquillo `x.xx.xx.` rotation 2 of E(5,8), the bossa clave rotation 10 of
E(5,16)...). **Clave Atlas**, 16 pulses: tresillo E(3,8), bossa clave E(5,16), cinquillo E(5,8), samba
E(7,16), a Central African E(9,16), calypso E(3,4); I-IV-V-IV in C. **Bembe Atlas**, 12 pulses (a 12/8):
the Ghanaian bell E(7,12) on a two-tone bell, the dancers' four E(4,12) on a djembe, the Venda clap
E(5,12), the Aka people's E(13,24) shaker and E(11,24) balafon line, a three-pulse bass E(3,12) against
the four. **Aksak Atlas**, odd cycles on one pulse: aksak E(4,9), ruchenitsa E(3,7), nawakhat E(5,7),
khafif-e-ramal E(2,5), agsag-samai E(5,9), Zappa's "Outside Now" E(4,11), in Hijaz Kar on D. Each
enters a phrase at a time, so the rhythms are heard alone before they stack.

Mipo Band's triangle was a single pitch and got old; a lone BELL now wanders with the jump macro
like a melody (a bell in a `pair`, Slow Brew's agogo, still keeps its two pitches). Note the cart
clamps melodic notes to 24..96 (quantize), which had been pinning the triangle at 96.

⚠ **An engine bug the singing ran into** (not fixed: `sound.h` is shared, and the fix changes
every voice cart): at note-on, `sound.h` runs `vox_apply_macros()` and THEN `sound_voice_start()`,
which resets the voice to a neutral "ah". So an `INSTR_VOICE` note started by `schedule_note_at` (or
`hit`/`note`) ignores its slot's harmonics/timbre/morph AND its NoteOpts offsets, and its shapes never
reach the vowel either; and a booked note cannot take `voice_consonant`. The workaround that worked
(before the singing was cut): queue each syllable and start it LIVE from `update()`, then set vowel,
size, effort and consonant through `note_harmonics/_timbre/_morph` + `voice_consonant` (measured: "pi"
~830 Hz centroid, "po" ~450 Hz, against ~630 Hz for every booked note). Fixing it = calling
`vox_apply_macros(v)` at the end of `sound_voice_start()`.

LEVELS in our biomes come from measured solo stems (`DE_DEFINES=DA_ID=<id>,DA_SOLO=<n>`), not the
factory fit: upstream's balance puts the quiet Plaits kick ~20 dB under a loud round bass, which a
four-on-the-floor cannot take. Kick and bass now sit within a few dB of each other.
