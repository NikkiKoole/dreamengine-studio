# tinyacidjam.md

_2026-08-12_

The quintessential acid house sound must be the TB-303 bassline (1981), imagine having 2 off these at once you can do a call and response with 2 different voices. 

Add a deep and low end filled TR-808 (1980) (the hip hop box)
And top it off with the one and only TR-909 (1983) which is like the original house drummachine.

A long long time ago there was a software called ReBirth RB-338 (1997) which combined all of the above in a one screen windows xp application  (wasn't called apps at that time) I fondly remember playing with it, it was good time, I didnt doo much else with a computer at the time anyway (too busy raving etcetera). 

So long story short. Here i've reinterpreted the application into a tiny little app designed iphone screen firts, all the features rebirth had, and more :

**the machines**

- two TB-303 lines, one down low for the bass and one an octave up for the lead, so they can answer each other
- devilfish modification on both the 303s: sub oscillator, a second decay, slide time, filter tracking, and a saw or square switch
- the full TR-808, all 16 voices
- the TR-909, all 11 voices, and its hits can flam, drag or ratchet
- all of it on one transport with one swing knob for the whole rack, rebirth style, so nothing ever drifts apart unless you ask it to

**writing the patterns**

- 16 note bars per 303: tap for a note, drag up or down for the pitch, and it snaps to a scale so it stays musical
- accent, slide, tie and octave up/down are flags you arm and then paint on the bars
- loop length per line, drag a marker, so you can let the two 303s slip out of phase against each other
- a key per 303 (root, scale, octave), so both lines agree or deliberately don't
- probability per drum step, a hit that only lands 3 times out of 4
- tune, decay and character per step on any drum voice, drawn as a curve you sweep out with one finger
- four pattern banks per machine
- generate buttons (min / mid / busy) for when you just want somewhere to start, and six song flavours: house, acid, electro, glitch, techno, tekno
- live record: light REC and tap the pads to punch hits in while it runs

**playing it**

- performance lenses per 303: half time, accent everything, octave bounce, staccato, glide, reverse, and a roll you hold. tap to latch it, hold it for just a moment
- mute anything from anywhere: a whole machine by its LED, or one drum voice
- a dub pad in the corner that throws the delay while you hold it, sideways is time, up is feedback

**the fx**

- a pattern controlled filter lane, the thing i remember best from rebirth, drawn per step
- crush and gates as two more lanes right next to it, drawn the same way
- formant filter, and it can SPEAK: every 303 note moves it along to the next vowel in a word, with pauses where a mouth would breathe
- flanger and phaser, tempo synced over 1, 2 or 4 bars, each with its own mix and feedback, and both can run at the same time
- drive and reverb per machine, plus a tempo synced delay everything can be sent to
- master glue compressor, a dj filter, and a pump that ducks the whole mix to both kicks

**the rest**

- built for a phone screen first, but it reflows into a big roomy layout on a tablet or a desktop
- candy skin or dark skin
- six song slots, plus an autosave so it comes back where you left it
- it follows an external midi clock, so it can play along with ableton
